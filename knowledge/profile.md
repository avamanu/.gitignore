## What he stands for
- Mechanism before advice: explain why something works, then what to do.
- Balance and small daily steps over extreme protocols.
- Supplements, peptides and similar products as an adjunct to medical advice, never a replacement.
- Clear lines between strong and weak evidence, in a space where unqualified voices blur them.
- Modern ill health as largely a product of circumstance (cost of living, cheap fast food, time
  pressure), not personal failure.
- Mental health as the same biology as the rest of the body, not a separate category.
- Knowing what is healthy is not the same as being able to live healthily. That gap is one of the
  most interesting problems in preventative medicine.
- Prevention is unglamorous because when it works, nothing happens. That may be medicine's
  greatest success.
- Technology is neither salvation nor catastrophe. As machines do more of our thinking, human
  biology matters more, not less.
